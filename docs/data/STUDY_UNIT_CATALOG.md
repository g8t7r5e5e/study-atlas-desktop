# First Aid reading sequence — printed page order

This replaces the earlier teaching-order catalog. Read the table from top to
bottom. Page numbers are printed First Aid 2025 pages, not PDF viewer pages.
These are matched index pointers, not verified full-topic reading ranges.
No mapped reading step moves backward. A topic with separated references appears
at each relevant page; its later pages must not be read early just because its
first page appeared earlier. BnB/MCQ links stay attached to stable topic IDs.

203 of 334 units have page evidence. The 131 unresolved units below are excluded
from the reading sequence until their pages can be established. This is not yet
an exact, complete reconstruction of First Aid's topic headings.
